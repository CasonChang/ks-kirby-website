## 2026-05-11

### expression | To be perfectly candid
坦白地說...（在可能尷尬的情境中表達完全的誠實）
> "To be perfectly candid, the food is a bit too salty for my taste."

### vocabulary | candid (adj.)
意思：坦率的、直白的
> 記憶技巧：聯想 "candid photo"（偷拍照）— 沒有擺姿勢、呈現真實的一面。

### grammar | Don't need to posing → You don't need to pose
規則：need to 後面接原形動詞 (base verb)，不是動名詞

### grammar | Be perfectly candid, I think... → To be perfectly candid, I think...
規則：開頭引導語要用不定詞片語 (infinitive phrase)，不是原形

### grammar | it's really close → Oh, it's so close! / That was almost it!
規則：表達「差一點點」時，母語者會用更生動的反應句，而不是直譯

### grammar | I have an example to using → I have an example of how to use
規則：example 後面接 of + how to + 動詞，不能直接用 to + V-ing

### grammar | It conbine → It combines
規則：第三人稱單數 (it) 後面動詞要加 s / es

### grammar | I'd go to sleep → I'm going to go to sleep / I'm heading to bed
規則：I'd 是 I would 的縮寫（表示假設或過去習慣），不是「我將要」的意思

## 2026-05-13

### vocabulary | streamline (v.)
意思：簡化/優化流程
> 記憶技巧：想像流線型設計，讓流程像水流一樣順暢。例句: "I want to build a tool to streamline the weekly reporting process."

### grammar | pioneer of the AI → pioneers in AI / a pioneer in the field of AI
規則：修正搭配詞，使用 "pioneer in [field]" 更為自然。

### grammar | study AI topics during working → study AI topics during work hours / while working
規則：修正時間表達，"during working" 並非標準用法。

### grammar | it's ok to me → it's okay with me / I'm fine with it
規則：表達同意或沒問題時，慣用 "okay with me"。

### grammar | provide to others collgues → provide it to my other colleagues / share it with my colleagues
規則：修正拼寫 (colleagues) 並補上受詞。

### grammar | makes work conveniently → makes work more convenient / makes work easier
規則：使役動詞 make + 受詞 + 形容詞 (convenient)，而非副詞 (conveniently)。

### grammar | write a letter with English → write a letter in English
規則：語言介質一律使用介系詞 "in"。

### grammar | meet the formal mail's format → follow a formal email format / match the required formal format
規則：格式建議使用 "follow" 或 "match"，而非 "meet"。

### grammar | The key to make presentation better is streamline → The key to making a presentation better is to streamline the content / streamlining
規則：'The key to' 後方必須接 V-ing 或名詞。
